Cinco esquinas: An Interactive Map

Every marker below is a real place in and around Lima that is named in Mario Vargas Llosa's Cinco esquinas (2016). Click a marker — or a place in the list — to read every sentence in the novel that mentions it.

This is the pilot dataset for A Literary Map of Peru: place mentions were first identified automatically with the rule-based extractor presented at WTC 2025, then manually verified and, where needed, corrected — each location below carries a human-checked place name, coordinate pair, and area-vs-point classification. A solid dot marks one precise spot (a hotel, a restaurant); a soft circle marks a broader area (a district, a city, the whole country). Areas nest inside each other — Cinco Esquinas is inside Barrios Altos, which is inside Lima, which is inside Perú — so the map only shows one level of that nesting at a time: zoom in and a broad area gives way to the smaller places inside it.
